# Brent × Iran Regime-Switching

MGTF 405 Business Forecasting — group final project (due **Mon Dec 7, 2026, 6 pm**).

Forecast weekly Brent oil volatility with a two-state Markov-switching model and test whether Iran-specific geopolitical risk explains or predicts switches into the crisis regime, beyond global geopolitical risk and market fear.
